Each member of your organization has a role for each product, and that role determines what the member can do in the product. This guide covers assigning and changing those roles from the **Members** tab of the **Organization** page.

For what each role includes, see [RBAC](/chainlink-platform/rbac). To add a new member to your organization, see [Inviting Team Members](/chainlink-platform/inviting-team-members).

## Prerequisites

- You must be logged in to <a href="https://app.chain.link" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">app.chain.link</a>.
- You must have a role that can manage member roles:
  - The **Owner** and **Platform Admins** can manage member roles for all products.
  - **Product Admins** (for example, a CRE Admin) can manage member roles for their product only.
- The member whose roles you want to change must already belong to your organization. If they don't, invite them first: see [Inviting Team Members](/chainlink-platform/inviting-team-members).

You can only set roles for products that you have access to.

## Adding or Modifying a member's roles

1. Sign in to <a href="https://app.chain.link" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">app.chain.link</a>.
1. Click your account icon in the top-right corner, then click **Org settings** to open the **Organization** page.

   <ClickToZoom
     src="/images/cre/UI/assigning-roles/1-navbar-organization.png"
     alt="Account menu with the Org settings option to navigate to app.chain.link/organization"
     style="max-width: 250px;"
   />

1. Open the **Members** tab. It lists every member of your organization.

   <ClickToZoom
     src="/images/cre/UI/assigning-roles/2-members-tab.png"
     alt="Members tab with the member list, Status and Product access filters, and Invite member button"
   />

1. Click the member's name to open the **Edit member form**.

1. On the **Roles** tab, open the dropdown next to a product and select the member's role for that product. The roles available in each dropdown depend on the product:

   <ClickToZoom
     src="/images/cre/UI/assigning-roles/3-role-dropdown.png"
     alt="Product role dropdown with the available roles listed"
     style="max-width: 500px;"
   />

   | Product      | Available roles                     |
   | ------------ | ----------------------------------- |
   | Platform     | Admin, User                         |
   | ACE          | Admin, Operator, Viewer, No Access  |
   | CRE          | Admin, Developer, Viewer, No Access |
   | CRE Connect  | Admin, Operator, Viewer, No Access  |
   | Data Streams | Admin, No Access                    |

   Set a product to **No Access** to remove the member's access to that product. The **Platform** role is required and has no **No Access** option.

1. To preview what a role grants before saving, expand the product's accordion. It shows the role's description and the permissions the role includes, grouped by service. Products set to **No Access** show **No role selected**.

   <ClickToZoom
     src="/images/cre/UI/assigning-roles/4-role-permissions.png"
     alt="Expanded role permissions accordion showing the role description and permissions grouped by service"
   />

1. Click **Save changes** to save your changes.
