To collaborate with your team on workflows, you can invite members to your organization. This guide walks you through the invitation process.

{/* prettier-ignore */}
<Aside type="note" title="Who can invite members">
  The first person to create an account for your organization automatically becomes the **Owner**. Members with the invite permission, such as Platform Admins and the Owner, can invite new members. See [RBAC](/chainlink-platform/rbac) for the full list of roles.
</Aside>

## Prerequisites

- You must be logged in to <a href="https://app.chain.link" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">app.chain.link</a>
- You must have a role that includes the invite permission, such as Platform Admin or Owner
- The email addresses you're inviting must belong to whitelisted domains

## Step 1: Navigate to Organization settings

In the top navbar, click on your account icon, and then navigate to **"Org settings"**.

<ClickToZoom
  src="/images/cre/UI/inviting-members/1-navbar-organization.png"
  alt="Organization menu in sidebar"
  style="max-width: 250px;"
/>

## Step 2: Go to the Members tab

Click on the **"Members"** tab to view your organization's members.

<ClickToZoom src="/images/cre/UI/inviting-members/2-members-tab.png" alt="Members tab" />

## Step 3: Start the invitation process

Click the **"Invite member"** link in the top right corner.

<ClickToZoom
  src="/images/cre/UI/inviting-members/3-invite-button.png"
  alt="Invite member link"
  style="max-width: 500px;"
/>

## Step 4: Add team member details

Enter the **email address** of the person you want to invite, and set their **roles** for each product. You can only set roles for products that you have access to. If you don't have access to a product, or your role lacks sufficient permissions for it, you won't be able to assign roles or permissions for that product.

<ClickToZoom src="/images/cre/UI/inviting-members/4-email-permissions.png" alt="Add name and email form" />

## Step 5: Send invitations

Once you've set all the roles you want to define for the invited member, click the **"Send invite"** button in the bottom right corner.

<ClickToZoom
  src="/images/cre/UI/inviting-members/5-submit-button.png"
  alt="Send invite button"
  style="max-width: 150px;"
/>

The invited members will receive an email invitation to join your organization.

## What happens next?

After you send invitations:

1. **Email notification**: Each invited person receives an email with instructions to join your organization
1. **Account creation**: If they don't have a Chainlink Platform account yet, they'll be invited to create one
1. **Automatic membership**: Once they complete account creation, they'll automatically become members of your organization. No additional acceptance step is required.
1. **Access**: They'll immediately have access to your organization's workflows and resources

<Aside type="note" title="Domain restrictions">
  The email addresses you invite must belong to domains that are whitelisted for CRE access. If an invited member's
  domain is not whitelisted, they won't be able to complete the account creation process.
</Aside>
