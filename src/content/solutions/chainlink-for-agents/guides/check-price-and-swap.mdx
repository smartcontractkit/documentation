<Aside type="note" title="Prerequisites">
1. This guide assumes that you have already linked your agent to the Chainlink platform, and have created a fresh wallet for it, using [Set up guardrails for your agent](/solutions/chainlink-for-agents/guides/set-guardrails). You also need:
    - A few USDC on Base left in your agent's address, to pay this guide's fees.
    - About 6 USDC on Base in the new chain wallet.
    - Your agent doesn't need any ETH: it pays its fees in USDC, and Chainlink for Agents sponsors the gas for its chain wallet.
1. This guide assumes you have set up appropriate guardrails relevant to your needs, as described in [Set up guardrails for your agent](/solutions/chainlink-for-agents/guides/set-guardrails#set-up-guardrails).

</Aside>

In this tutorial, you will:

1. Have your agent fetch live token prices from Chainlink's Data Streams.
1. Swap USDC for ETH on Base through Uniswap.
1. Check your agent's balances after the swap.
1. Watch a guardrail block a swap.

<Aside type="tip" title="Agent address vs. chain wallet">
  Your agent works with two addresses:

- **Agent address:** the address your agent created during sign-up, and the one you linked to your organization. It's your agent's identity, and your agent pays its Chainlink for Agents fees from it.
- **Chain wallet:** a [smart contract wallet](/crec/concepts/smart-accounts) that Chainlink for Agents deploys for your agent on each chain it uses, such as Base. It holds the tokens your agent swaps or sends. It has no private key of its own: it only carries out instructions signed with your agent's key and delivered by Chainlink for Agents.
  - The Chainlink platform and the skills repo also call it an SVA (Signature Verifying Account).

Your agent's swaps run from its chain wallet, so Chainlink for Agents can check each one against your guardrails before it goes onchain. Your link covers both addresses, but guardrails only apply to what your agent does from its chain wallet.

</Aside>

## Check the ETH price

This serves as the first test of your agent's connection to the Chainlink platform.

Give this prompt to your agent:

```
What's the current price of ETH in US dollars? Get it from Chainlink Data Streams through Chainlink for Agents.
```

The agent should be able to fetch the latest price straight from the platform via Chainlink Data Streams.
Simply reading the data doesn't require you to set up a separate chain wallet, but the next step does.

## Swap USDC for ETH using Uniswap

In [Set up guardrails](/solutions/chainlink-for-agents/guides/set-guardrails#set-up-guardrails), you allowed your agent's chain wallet to swap tokens on Uniswap. This swap confirms that your guardrails allow it.

Give this prompt to your agent to initiate the swap:

```
Swap 5 USDC for ETH on Base from your chain wallet.

Before you sign, show me what I'll get and wait for my confirmation.
```

> You can also edit the prompt to have your agent simply execute the swap without asking for confirmation.

- Before moving forward, ask your agent to check if the swap went through, and make sure your balances reflect the updated amounts.

## Watch a guardrail block a swap

1. Go to the [Chainlink platform's Agents dashboard](https://app.chain.link/cre/agents), and locate your agent.
1. Click on the **Settings** icon and configure your existing settings to turn off **Uniswap - token swaps**. Click on **Apply Settings**.
1. Paste this prompt to try and swap your remaining `USDC` for `ETH` **after turning** off Uniswap swaps from inside the UI.

   ```
   Swap 1 USDC for ETH on Base please.
   ```

   Your agent should now refuse to execute the swap, because Uniswap swaps are no longer allowed. It may still pay the small quote fee before the swap is blocked.

## What's next

You've seen your guardrails allow one swap and block the next. From here, try changing one setting at a time and asking your agent for something new. Keep the amounts small while you experiment.

Some ideas:

- **Earn yield on Aave**: Turn on the **Aave - deposits and withdrawals** setting, then ask your agent to interact with the protocol.
- **Bridge using CCIP**: Turn on the **Bridge using CCIP** setting and try a cross-chain token transfer. You can also turn on the **Transfer and Bridge Allowlist** setting and try bridging tokens to an address that isn't on the allowlist.

To see everything else your agent can do, go back to [What can your agent do](/solutions/chainlink-for-agents#what-can-your-agent-do).
