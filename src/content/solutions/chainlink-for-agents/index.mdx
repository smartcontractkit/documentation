Chainlink for Agents is a service we built specifically for AI agents, built on [CRE Connect](/crec). \
Your AI agent uses the service to sign up to our platform, pay fees, and runs workflows automatically.

You use the Chainlink platform UI, a separate web app, to link your agent to your organization and set the guardrails it works within.

## What can your agent do

Your agent uses the Chainlink catalog, within the guardrails you set. The catalog lets your agent:

- Get current and past prices from [Chainlink Data Streams](/data-streams).
- Look up token details and check its token balances.
- Send tokens such as USDC.
- Swap tokens through Uniswap.
- Check Aave yields and its own positions, and supply tokens to Aave or withdraw them.
- Bridge tokens to another chain with [Chainlink CCIP](/ccip).

## Who is this for

This service is targeted towards, but not limited to, people who want an AI agent to work with Chainlink for them, without writing code.

> Your agent gets ready-made workflows and a way to pay for them, and you still get to set guardrails that your agent can't change.

Your agent works autonomously, yet still operates within the parameters you set for it.

## How your agent connects to Chainlink

You and your agent go through four phases during the linkup process:

1. **Sign up:** You give your agent the skills repo link, and your agent creates its own signing key.
   The key's address is your agent's identity, and the key never leaves your agent's machine. Your agent pays its fees from that address, so it asks you to fund it; a few USDC on Base covers sign-up and your first workflows.
   Once the USDC arrives, your agent accepts the [Chainlink Terms of Service](https://chain.link/terms) on your behalf, pays a small sign-up fee, and registers with Chainlink for Agents. Its registration limits it to workflows from the Chainlink catalog.

2. **Link Up:** In the [Chainlink platform](https://app.chain.link/cre/discover), you enter your agent's address and generate a one-time token that uniquely identifies your organization within the platform UI.
   You give this generated token with your agent, which signs it and gives you back a signature. You paste that signature back into the platform. The signature proves that your agent holds the key for that address, so the platform links your agent to your organization.

3. **Set guardrails.** In the Chainlink platform, you choose which kinds of actions your agent may take: sending, swapping or bridging tokens, and using Aave. Or maybe you want your agent to not want to do any of those actions? You can set that up too.
   You also choose which wallets your agent may send or bridge tokens to, and how much it may send or bridge per transaction and per rolling 24 hours. Your agent can't change these settings, only you can, using the UI.

4. **Run workflows.** Your agent runs workflows through Chainlink for Agents and pays a small fee for each one. Workflows that move tokens use a chain wallet, a separate wallet your agent creates on each chain. Chainlink for Agents checks every workflow against your guardrails, checks again just before anything is written onchain, and blocks any workflow that breaks a guardrail.

<Aside type="caution" title="Please Note">
  Without guardrails, your agent can run any workflow in the catalog, with any token, recipient, or bridge destination,
  and no spend limit. Set guardrails that match what you want your agent to do.
</Aside>
