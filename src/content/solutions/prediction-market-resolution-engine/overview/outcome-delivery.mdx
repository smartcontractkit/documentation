Chainlink delivers each market's outcome to your platform. How it is delivered is set in your agreement with Chainlink, not through the Market Registry API: the API is a catalog and does not return outcomes.

## What is delivered

Each market resolves to one of three outcomes:

| Outcome | Meaning                                                                          |
| ------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `YES`   | The market's condition held.                                                     |
| `NO`    | The market's condition did not hold.                                             |
| `PUSH`  | A tie, on a market whose rule pays out 50/50 on a tie. Both sides are paid half. |

A market whose evidence never supports an answer receives no outcome. An absent outcome is not `NO`.

## Where outcomes go

Chainlink sends outcomes to the destination agreed for your platform: an API your platform operates, or a contract. The delivery format, credentials, and market identifiers are set up with Chainlink during onboarding.

If your platform holds outcomes for a dispute window before they are final, delivery follows that window in two steps:

1. When the outcome is determined, Chainlink sends it as an indicative outcome. The event's status becomes `final`.
1. After the dispute window, Chainlink sends the final outcome. It reuses the outcome and evidence already decided; providers are not queried again, so the final outcome cannot drift from the indicative one. The event's status becomes `completed`.

## Follow progress in the registry

The Market Registry API does not return outcomes, but each event's `event_status` shows where resolution stands:

| `event_status` | Delivery                                                    |
| -------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| `live`         | No outcome yet. The event is in progress.                   |
| `unsafe`       | No outcome. The evidence cannot support an answer yet.      |
| `final`        | Outcomes are determined. Delivery can still be in progress. |
| `completed`    | Resolution and delivery are complete.                       |
| `cancelled`    | The event will not occur. No outcome is delivered.          |

See [Track event status](/solutions/prediction-market-resolution-engine/tutorial/track-event-status) to read these statuses from the API.

## One answer for every platform

A market's slug identifies the underlying question. When several platforms list the same market, the engine decides the outcome once and delivers the same outcome to each platform.
