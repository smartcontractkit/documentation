Chainlink Data Streams offers two families for US single-name equities and ETFs: [24/5 streams](/data-streams/rwa-streams/24-5-us-equities-user-guide) and [24/7 streams](/data-streams/rwa-streams/24-7-us-equities). Both cover US equities, but they differ in how sessions are handled, which fields you consume, and what happens when the market is closed.

## 24/5 vs 24/7 <span id="24-5-vs-24-7"></span>

The table below compares the two families on the details that apply across both: stream count, session handling, weekends and holidays, and the price fields they publish.

|                       | [24/5 US Equities](/data-streams/rwa-streams/24-5-us-equities-user-guide) | [24/7 US Equities](/data-streams/rwa-streams/24-7-us-equities)   |
| :-------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------ | :--------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Streams per asset     | Three (Regular, Extended, Overnight)                                      | One per product                                                  |
| Session switching     | Integrator chooses the session stream using `marketStatus`                | No session switching; the stream publishes continuously          |
| Weekends and holidays | Market closed; prices resume at the next open                             | Continues publishing through weekends and holidays               |
| Price fields          | `mid`, `bid`, `ask` per session                                           | TWAP: single `price`<br />Perpetual Markets: `mid`, `bid`, `ask` |

For field-level behavior by product, see the [24/7 US Equities User Guide](/data-streams/rwa-streams/24-7-us-equities).

## Which should I use?

- **[24/5](/data-streams/rwa-streams/24-5-us-equities-user-guide#available-streams)** if you want session-level control and full control over how regular, extended, and overnight prices are combined.
- **[24/7 for Perpetual Markets](/data-streams/rwa-streams/24-7-us-equities#24-7-for-perpetual-markets)** if you need a continuous fast price (`mid`, `bid`, `ask`) through weekends and holidays.
- **[24/7 TWAP](/data-streams/rwa-streams/24-7-us-equities#24-7-twap)** if you need a single continuous TWAP price per asset.

For lending, use [24/7 Smoothed Tokenized Equity Feeds](/data-feeds/tokenized-equity-feeds/24-7), which publish the slow price onchain.
