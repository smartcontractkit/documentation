Chainlink Data Streams offers two families for US single-name equities and ETFs: **24/5 streams** and **24/7 streams**. Both cover the same US equity market, but they differ in how sessions are handled, which fields you consume, and what happens when the market is closed.

## 24/5 vs 24/7 <span id="24-5-vs-24-7"></span>

|                       | 24/5 US Equities                                              | 24/7 US Equities                                               |
| :-------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------ | :------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Streams per asset     | Three (Regular, Extended, Overnight)                          | One per product                                                |
| Session switching     | Integrator chooses the stream using `marketStatus`            | Handled by the stream                                          |
| Weekends and holidays | Market closed (`marketStatus = 5`). Prices resume at the open | Off-hours price from perpetual futures (`marketStatus = 5`)    |
| Session transitions   | Each session's price as published, so you control transitions | Smoothed by the stream, except pre-market into regular hours   |
| `lastTradedPrice`     | Deprecated                                                    | Carries the slow price (Oracle Price)                          |
| Asset coverage        | Broad coverage of US single-name equities and ETFs            | Assets reviewed for off-hours liquidity                        |
| Latency               | Lowest available for each session                             | Slightly higher, due to the additional stitching and smoothing |

This table describes the v11 Perpetual Markets and Lending streams. 24/7 TWAP streams use v2 and publish a single TWAP price.

The 24/5 streams remain available and are still the right choice if you want session-level data and full control over how sessions are combined.

## Which should I use?

- **[24/5](/data-streams/rwa-streams/24-5-us-equities-user-guide#available-streams)** if you want session-level control and full control over how regular, extended, and overnight prices are combined.
- **[24/7 for Perpetual Markets](/data-streams/rwa-streams/24-7-us-equities#24-7-for-perpetual-markets)** if you need a continuous fast price (`mid`, `bid`, `ask`) through weekends and holidays.
- **[24/7 for Lending](/data-streams/rwa-streams/24-7-us-equities#24-7-for-lending)** if you need a continuous slow price (`lastTradedPrice`, the Oracle Price) for collateral and liquidations.
- **[24/7 TWAP](/data-streams/rwa-streams/24-7-us-equities#24-7-twap)** if you need a single continuous TWAP price per asset.
