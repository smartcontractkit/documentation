These guides cover specific Data Streams markets and instruments, including how each stream is structured, how its fields behave across sessions, and the risks to account for when integrating.

Start with the guide for the market you're integrating. It covers the data you receive and what your protocol is responsible for.

| Guide                                                    | Covers                                                                      |
| :------------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [US Equities](/data-streams/rwa-streams/us-equities)     | 24/5 and 24/7 US equity streams, including TWAP and perpetual markets       |
| [APAC Equities](/data-streams/rwa-streams/apac-equities) | Asia-Pacific indices and single-name stocks during each exchange's sessions |
| [Futures](/data-streams/rwa-streams/futures)             | US oil, UK oil, and natural gas futures                                     |

## Not sure where to start?

- Looking for a stream ID or report schema? See [Streams & Schemas](/data-streams/reference/report-schema-overview) or the [RWA stream directory](/data-streams/rwa-streams).
- Looking for code to fetch, stream, decode, or verify reports? See [Integration Tutorials](/data-streams/tutorials/overview).
- Looking for API or SDK details? See [API & SDK Reference](/data-streams/reference/overview).
- Integrating on Canton? See [Canton Integration](/data-streams/canton-integration).

For schema-level market-hours and corporate-action guidance, see [Handling Market Events (RWA)](/data-streams/rwa-streams/handling-market-events).
