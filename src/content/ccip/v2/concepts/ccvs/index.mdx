Cross-Chain Verifiers (CCVs) are CCIP's pluggable verification layer. Each CCV attests that a source-chain message is valid before the destination chain executes it.

Most integrations use CCIP's default Committee Verifier without additional configuration. The pages below cover the verification models, interfaces and guarantees, and the trust and responsibility model for CCVs.
