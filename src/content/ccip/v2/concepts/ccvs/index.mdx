Cross-Chain Verifiers (CCVs) are CCIP's pluggable verification layer. Each CCV attests that a source-chain message is valid before the destination chain executes it.

Most integrations use CCIP's default CommitteeVerifier without additional configuration.
The underlying section goes into detail on the different verification models, interfaces, guarantees, and the trust and responsibility model for CCVs.
