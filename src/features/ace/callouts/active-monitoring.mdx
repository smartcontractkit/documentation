<Aside type="note" title="Preventive and continuous compliance">
  ACE enforces compliance in two ways. Policy Manager and Identity Manager are **preventive**: they block a transaction
  while it executes. [Active Monitoring](/ace/active-monitoring/overview) is **continuous**: it screens addresses on a
  schedule and acts onchain on tokens you already run, without changing their contracts. See [Preventive and Continuous
  Compliance](/ace/concepts/preventive-vs-continuous) to choose between them.
</Aside>
