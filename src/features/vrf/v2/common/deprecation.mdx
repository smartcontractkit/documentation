<Aside type="danger" title="Chainlink VRF v2 shuts down on November 30, 2026">
  VRF v2 was deprecated on November 29, 2024 and will be permanently shut down on November 30, 2026. A 48-hour
  brownout is scheduled for October 28-30, 2026, during which VRF v2 will not fulfill randomness requests. After the
  shutdown date, VRF v2 will no longer fulfill randomness requests. [Migrate your application to Chainlink VRF
  v2.5](/vrf/v2-5/migration-from-v2) before November 30, 2026 to avoid service disruption.

The migration guide includes VRF v2.5 code examples for both subscription and direct funding and explains the
differences between VRF v2.5 and VRF v2.

If you have questions about this shutdown or need help with your migration, reach out to your Chainlink
representative.

_Continued use of VRF v2 is at your own risk. Use of the Chainlink Network is subject to the [Chainlink Foundation Terms of Service](https://chain.link/terms), which provides important information and disclosures._

</Aside>
