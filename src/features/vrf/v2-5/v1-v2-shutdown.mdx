<Aside type="danger" title="VRF v1 and v2 shut down in November 2026">
VRF v1 and v2 were deprecated on November 29, 2024 and will be permanently shut down in November 2026. VRF v2 has a
48-hour brownout scheduled for October 28-30, 2026. VRF v1 shuts down on November 18, 2026 and VRF v2 shuts down on
November 30, 2026. After these dates, VRF v1 and v2 will no longer fulfill randomness requests.

[Migrate your application to Chainlink VRF v2.5](/vrf/v2-5/migration-from-v2) to avoid service disruption.

If you have questions about this shutdown or need help with your migration, reach out to your Chainlink
representative.

</Aside>
